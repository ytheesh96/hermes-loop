# Learning Record Format

```markdown
# Learning Record: YYYY-MM-DD — [Session]

## Work Attempted
- [Task or question]

## Demonstrated Evidence
- [What the learner produced unaided or with support]

## Assistance Required
- none | cue | worked example | step-by-step support

## Misconceptions and Open Questions
- [Observed issue, not a personality label]

## Resources Used
- [Link to RESOURCES.md entry]

## Retrieval Targets
- [Concept and suggested revisit point]

## Proposed Next Step
- [Small mission-linked action]
```

Distinguish introduced, practiced, and demonstrated. Never infer mastery from exposure alone.
