# Lesson Format

```markdown
# Lesson N: [Title]

## Mission Link
[Outcome advanced]

## Retrieval Warm-up
- [Prompt from prior learning]

## New Step
[Concise explanation with citations]

## Worked Example
[Concrete demonstration]

## Learner Task
[Predict, explain, solve, compare, or build]

## Feedback Criteria
[What evidence counts and common misconception]

## Transfer Prompt
[Different surface context]

## Retrieval Targets
- [What to revisit later]

## Sources
- [Citation or URL]
```

Keep active learner work larger than passive exposition.
